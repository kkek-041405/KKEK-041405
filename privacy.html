PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 8, 2026 • Applies to "The Chess Game" & KKEK Mobile Applications

01. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Konatham Komal Eshwara Kumar (KKEK) ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with our mobile applications, including The Chess Game (Package: com.KKEK.chess) available on Google Play, and our website at https://www.kkek.dev.

We believe in privacy by design. Our applications are built with a local-first architecture, meaning your gameplay data, move history, and rating records stay on your own device.

02. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

A. Local Gameplay Data (Stored Locally on Device)

When you play matches, the application stores your game state, move history, match outcomes (win/loss/draw), and calculated Elo rating locally using an on-device SQLite database. This data is entirely stored in your device's private app sandbox and is never transmitted to our servers or sold to third parties.

B. Personal Information

We do not require you to register an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or contacts to play chess.

C. Diagnostic & Technical Logs

If an unexpected application crash occurs, Google Play Services may collect standard anonymous diagnostic telemetry (such as device model, OS version, and crash stack traces) in accordance with Google Play's privacy terms to help us identify and fix bugs.

03. Device Permissions

The Chess Game is designed with minimal permission requirements:

  • Network State (INTERNET): Used solely for checking standard app updates or future online matchmaking.
  • No Invasive Permissions: We do not request or access camera, microphone, device location, contacts, calendar, or external storage.

04. Third-Party Services

Our application may utilize trusted third-party developer services provided by Google:

  • Google Play Services (License verification and in-app updates)

These third-party providers have their own privacy policies addressing how they handle data.

05. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our games and applications are family-friendly and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

06. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your match history and rating stats are saved locally on your Android device, you have complete control over your data:

  • You can clear match history directly within app settings or via Android system settings (Settings > Apps > Chess > Storage > Clear Data).
  • Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all stored game records and statistics.

07. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL (https://www.kkek.dev/privacy) with an updated revision date.
